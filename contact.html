Let’s talk

If your data doesn’t feel reliable — there is usually a reason behind it.

When does it make sense to reach out?

Most companies don’t realize they have a data problem until it starts affecting decisions.

If any of the situations below sound familiar, it might be a good time to start a conversation:

  • Reports don’t match and no one can clearly explain why
  • Different teams use different definitions for the same data
  • There is no clear ownership of data
  • Data quality issues keep repeating
  • Decisions are based more on assumptions than facts

These are not technical problems. They are control and governance problems.
