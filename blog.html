Data Governance in Practice

Data Governance is often misunderstood as documentation or control. In reality, it is about creating a system where data is trusted, structured, and aligned with business processes.

Most organizations struggle not because they lack data, but because they lack consistency. Different systems, different rules, and no clear ownership lead to unreliable reporting and poor decision-making.

In practice, Data Governance means defining ownership, implementing validation rules, and ensuring that data flows are aligned across systems, from source to reporting.

The goal is simple: turn data into a reliable asset that supports business decisions, not something that creates uncertainty.

Data Governance u praksi

Data Governance se često pogrešno posmatra kao dokumentacija ili kontrola. U realnosti, to je sistem koji obezbeđuje da su podaci pouzdani, strukturirani i usklađeni sa biznis procesima.

Većina kompanija nema problem sa nedostatkom podataka, već sa njihovom nekonzistentnošću. Različiti sistemi, različita pravila i nepostojanje jasnog vlasništva dovode do nepouzdanih izveštaja.

U praksi, Data Governance znači definisanje vlasništva nad podacima, implementaciju validacionih pravila i usklađivanje tokova podataka kroz sve sisteme.

Cilj je jednostavan: pretvoriti podatke u pouzdan alat za donošenje odluka, a ne izvor nesigurnosti.
