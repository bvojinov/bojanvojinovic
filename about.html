About me

I help companies bring control, structure and trust to their data.

Data Governance is not about documents. It is about decisions.

I am a Data Governance and Data Quality professional focused on helping companies make their data more reliable, understandable and useful for business decision-making.

My work sits between business, data and technology. I focus on translating complex data problems into clear governance structures, ownership models, validation rules and reporting logic that people can actually use.

I believe that companies do not become data-driven just by having more reports. They become data-driven when they understand where the data comes from, who owns it, how it is controlled and why it can be trusted.
