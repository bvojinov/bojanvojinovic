About me

Data Governance, Data Quality and the business side of trusted data.

Data Governance is not only about data. It is about responsibility.

I write about Data Governance because I believe data problems are rarely just technical problems.

Behind every inconsistent report, unclear definition or repeated data quality issue, there is usually a deeper problem: missing ownership, weak control or a process that was never clearly defined.

My professional background connects finance, retail, reporting, data quality and governance. That mix shaped the way I think about data: not as something that belongs only to IT, but as a business asset that needs structure, responsibility and trust.

How I think about data

01. If no one owns the data, no one is truly responsible for it.

02. A report is only as reliable as the process behind it.

03. Data Quality is not only a technical topic — it is a business discipline.

04. Governance must be practical, visible and connected to real decisions.

05. The goal is not control for the sake of control — the goal is trust.
